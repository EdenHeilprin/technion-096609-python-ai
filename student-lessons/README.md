# Student lessons

Lessons prepared and taught by student pairs in Classes 11–13. Each lesson focuses on one useful topic, with an example and an audience activity.

Browse accepted lessons in the **[student-lessons folder](https://github.com/EdenHeilprin/technion-096609-python-ai/tree/main/student-lessons)**. Open a pair's folder to read its lesson and find its files. Proposed lessons remain under the course's [Pull requests](https://github.com/EdenHeilprin/technion-096609-python-ai/pulls) until reviewed and merged.

To contribute, follow **[Class 10 — Create and Contribute Your Own Lesson](../class-10/README.md)**. Agree on a topic with the instructor first so each pair teaches something different. Introduce the topic through a live example and a small activity, aiming for familiarity rather than mastery. Keep your contribution inside your own folder; you do not need to edit this index.
