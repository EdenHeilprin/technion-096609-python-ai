# Classes 11–13 — Student-led Lessons

In Classes 11–13, each pair will teach an approximately **30-minute lesson** on an approved topic. Introduce one useful idea through a live example and a small activity or discussion. Both partners present.

Accepted lessons will appear as folders here. Open a pair's folder to read its lesson and find its files. Lessons awaiting review appear under the course's [Pull requests](https://github.com/EdenHeilprin/technion-096609-python-ai/pulls).

To contribute, follow **[Class 10 — Create and Contribute Your Own Lesson](../class-10/README.md)**. Agree on a topic with the instructor first so each pair teaches something different. Introduce the topic through a live example and a small activity, aiming for familiarity rather than mastery. Keep your contribution inside your own folder; you do not need to edit this index.
