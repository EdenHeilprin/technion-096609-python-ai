# Classes 11–12 — Student-led Lessons

In Classes 11–12, prepare a **40–50-minute lesson** on a selected topic, normally in pairs. Individual presentations are possible by prior agreement. Introduce one useful idea through a live example and an activity or discussion. In a pair, both partners present.

Presentations take place on **Friday, 27 November (Zoom)** and **Wednesday, 2 December (in person)**. Follow [Class 10](../class-10/README.md) for topic selection and signup instructions. Submit your lesson for review through a pull request by **25 November at 14:00 (Israel time)**.

Accepted lessons will appear as folders here. Open a lesson's folder to read it and find its files. Lessons awaiting review appear under the course's [Pull requests](https://github.com/EdenHeilprin/technion-096609-python-ai/pulls).

To contribute, follow **[Class 10 — Create and Contribute Your Own Lesson](../class-10/README.md)**. Reserve an unused topic in the signup sheet so each lesson introduces something different. Introduce the topic through a live example and a small activity, aiming for familiarity rather than mastery. Keep your contribution inside your own folder; you do not need to edit this index.
