A short classroom study

Six decisions. Your preferences.

In each round, choose between a guaranteed number of points and a gamble: a 50% chance of 10 points or a 50% chance of 0 points.

Then tell us how confident you are in your choice. There are no right or wrong answers.

The points are hypothetical: no money is paid. We do not ask for your name. Your responses will be discussed as a group in class.
