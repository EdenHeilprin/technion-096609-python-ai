## The experiment in Classes 8 and 9

How much extra expected value do people require to choose a gamble over a sure outcome? Participants make **seven decisions** between a sure **10 points** and a **50% chance of a varying prize**, otherwise zero. They are randomly assigned to ascending or descending prize order. The points are hypothetical.

Class 8 supplies the experiment brief and preregistration for a fresh Codex project. Class 9 supplies the working experiment so you can test your setup without generating it again.
